Segment contribution and concentration

Operating-group profit growth was led by CGM and transaction gains

CGM

Largest uplift: OnStream and Asset Finance gains, stronger gas, power and oil hedging, and North American supply-demand trading opportunities; partly offset by platform investment, brokerage costs and impairments.

MAM

Profit rose on performance fees and gains from selling the North American and European Public Investments business; lower base fees and the absence of the prior-year Rotorcraft gain reduced the benefit.

Macquarie Capital

Growth came from infrastructure and technology equity exits, private-credit income, Americas and ANZ M&A fees, and Asian brokerage; impairments and associate/JV losses were offsets.

BFS

Higher average loan, deposit and platform balances lifted net interest and fee income, partly absorbed by margin pressure from portfolio mix and competition plus higher technology spend.

Corporate

Wider net expense reflected higher profit share and tax, Green Investment impairments and the non-recurrence of prior realisations, partly offset by lower Green Investment platform spend.

Audited Note 4, pages 186-187; Management commentary (Macquarie) pages 29-31.

Statement of financial position

Assets and liabilities expanded through loans, deposits and derivatives

Asset movements Total assets

Liability movements Total liabilities

Total assets
FY2025

on prior year

Principal drivers: loan assets increased from BFS home loans and CGM corporate, commercial and other lending; derivative assets rose with gas and power price volatility.

Also contributing: reverse repos from Treasury liquidity management and CGM trading, financial investments from larger high-quality liquid asset portfolios, and trading assets from listed equity securities in CGM.

Total liabilities
FY2025

on prior year

Principal drivers: deposits grew in BFS; derivative liabilities increased with derivative asset movements; issued debt and borrowings rose from commercial paper, certificates of deposit and Treasury borrowings.

Also contributing: higher margin and settlement balances, and increased short positions in listed equity securities in CGM.

Total equity
FY2025

on prior year

Increased by earnings: current-year profit added to equity.

Partly offset by: dividend payments and a lower foreign-currency translation reserve, largely driven by Australian-dollar appreciation against the US dollar.

Audited page 173; Management commentary (Macquarie) page 32.
